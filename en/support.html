How do I enter scores?

Choose 301 or 501, add 2–8 players and enter each dart. Use Double, Triple, 25, Bull 50 or Miss. Undo corrects the last entry.

How does the trial work?

Three full matches are free. One permanent purchase then unlocks unlimited new matches. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the same Apple Account as for the purchase, then open Settings > Services and purchases > Restore purchases. Internet access may be required.

How do I move to another device?

Export a backup from Settings > Backup and restore, transfer the file and import it on the new device. Restore the purchase separately with the same Apple Account.

How can I report a problem?

Email us with your device model, iOS version and the steps to reproduce the issue. Never send passwords or bank information.
