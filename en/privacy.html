Privacy

Updated: 22 October 2026

Your games stay on your device

Player names, darts, scores, match history and backups are stored locally. We do not send them to our analytics services. You can export them to Files or share them yourself. A second score display uses the local network.

One-time purchase

Apple processes payment. RevenueCat receives transaction information and a pseudonymous installation identifier to validate purchases and restore permanent access. We do not receive your card details. No player account or subscription is required.

Analytics and diagnostics

PostHog receives usage events, aggregate counts, rule options, a pseudonymous identifier and technical app and device information. Sentry receives crash and performance reports and technical diagnostic information. Dartpop does not send player names, backups, screenshots or session recordings. PostHog geolocation and advertising tracking are disabled.

Providers and retention

Technical data is processed by RevenueCat, PostHog and Sentry under their policies and configured retention periods. These providers may process data outside your country. Local data remains until removed from the device. Exported backups remain under your control.

Your choices and rights

Contact us to request information, access or deletion of technical data relating to you. Never send passwords or payment details. Deleting the app removes its local files; exported backups must be removed separately. The trial counter may remain in the iOS keychain and is not reset by importing a backup.
